Dunning copy templates

Purpose: Calm, actionable messaging for failed payments. Avoid alarmist language.

First failure (automated retry scheduled)
- Title: "Payment failed"
- Body: "We couldn't process the most recent payment for this subscription. We'll retry automatically — updating your payment method now will prevent future attempts."
- CTA: "Fix payment method"

Second failure (after first retry)
- Title: "Payment still didn't go through"
- Body: "We attempted another charge but it didn't succeed. Update your payment method to avoid service interruptions."
- CTA: "Update payment method"

Final warning (last retry failed)
- Title: "Action needed: Payment unsuccessful"
- Body: "This is the last retry. To keep this subscription active, update the payment method now or contact support for assistance."
- CTA: "Fix payment method"

Tone notes:
- Use neutral language: "couldn't process" not "failed" when possible.
- Focus on next steps and assistance; include support link if helpful.
