# Landing pricing calculator

## Overview

The landing pricing calculator provides an accessible preview of an expected monthly spend using three adjustable inputs: seats, calls, and storage. It is embedded on the landing experience so prospects can estimate the cost before they decide on a plan.

## Accessibility notes

- Inputs are keyboard accessible with visible labels and numeric fallbacks.
- Range sliders expose `aria-label` and `aria-describedby` metadata.
- The summary panel announces the recommended plan and is linked to the pricing section.

## Responsive behavior

- The layout stacks vertically on smaller viewports and shifts to a two-column arrangement on larger screens.
- Numeric inputs remain usable when precise values are required.
